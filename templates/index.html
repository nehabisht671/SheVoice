ABOUT SHEVOICE

Because Every Woman Deserves To Be Heard.

SheVoice is a web-based grievance management platform designed to provide students with a structured and confidential way to raise concerns within their university.

Through complaint submission, committee assignment and status tracking, SheVoice aims to make grievance management more transparent, accessible and organized.
